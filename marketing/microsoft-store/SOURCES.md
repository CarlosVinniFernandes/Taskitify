## Real captures (raw-captures/desktop)

Taken on 2026-11-27 on a real Windows 11 PC (1920 × 1200, 100 %), on a temporary virtual desktop with a temporary Edge profile.
Account pictures, the account name and Excel's activation notice are covered in the composition (`desktop()` in `source/compose.py`).

- `browse-1.png`: Microsoft Edge, Bing search "best places to visit in Italy". The search results show third-party thumbnails at small size.
- `study-1.png`: Microsoft Edge, Wikipedia article "History of coffee" (text CC BY-SA 4.0; the painting *The Coffee Bearer*, 1857, is public domain).
- `work-1.png`: Microsoft Excel with a fictional project budget (names and figures invented).
- `relax-1.png`, `desk-fuji.png`, `desk-clean.png`: the desktop and taskbar.

The Windows UI on this PC is in Portuguese (taskbar language indicator, date format, Excel ribbon); web pages are in English.

## Taskitify components (raw-captures/components)

Rendered from the app's production WPF views and Lyrics Studio renderer by `source/Campaign.cs`. Every song title, lyric line and cover
is original demonstration material written for this campaign ("After the Rain", "Midnight Echo", "Golden Hour", "Paper Planes",
"Northern Lines" by "Demo Artist"). The taskbar players are placed where Taskitify docks on that taskbar (just left of the overflow chevron).

Lyrics Studio cards show the lyrics provider credit the renderer adds ("Spotify"); remove it before submission if the Store reviewer objects to other products' names.
